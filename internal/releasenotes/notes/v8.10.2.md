Two security fixes in the image, and one change aimed at the green flash that is a hypothesis rather than a repair.

## 🔒 Security

- **rclone moves to 1.75.1**, which closes two vulnerabilities the image scanner flags as critical. They were the only two of the image's five criticals that are ours to move; the other three come from Perl in the Debian base. Both checksums are taken from rclone's own published sums for that release, and the build verifies them before installing.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Animations at "Wild" no longer fade on a spring** ([#228](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/228)). A spring curve drives its value past the target and back, which is the point for a distance and meaningless for opacity: the value stops at fully visible, so the fade finished early and then sat still for the rest of the animation. The movement keeps its spring; the fade now runs on a curve that only eases.
- **This is aimed at the green flash and is not claimed as its fix.** What the report narrowed down: "Off" and "Subtle" are clean, "Wild" is not, and the reporter is already rendering in software, so the graphics card is out. The remaining difference between those settings is that curve. That makes it the change worth making, and it is still a hypothesis. The issue stays open until somebody has tested it.
- **A duplicate animation definition** in the stylesheet, declared twice identically, so the second one silently won.
