## 🐛 Fixed

- **The page entrance no longer scales or tilts** ([#228](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/228)). v8.10.0 moved the default down to "Subtle", where neither happens. That kept a new install away from the symptom and left it untouched for anybody who had chosen "Wild", because a changed default never reaches a setting you picked yourself.
- **What was believed at the time, and is not established.** A page that slides can be drawn independently of its contents; one that scales or rotates cannot, so the browser redraws everything inside it through the page's own transform while the cards in there animate too. That was the reasoning. It does not survive the arithmetic: `#38FF38` is rgb(56, 255, 56), with red and blue exactly equal and green clipped, which is the shape of one wrong byte rather than of uninitialised memory. Whether the wrong pixel is produced in the page or in the compositor cannot be decided by reading the source.
- **"Wild" is still clearly livelier than "Subtle"**: three times the travel, longer, on a curve that overshoots and settles. None of that asks the browser to redraw a page.
- **"Storm" lost the largest values in the file** (3 degrees, 90%). Since it began overriding the system's reduced-motion setting in v8.10.0, it is the one level that animates for somebody who asked for less movement, so it is the last place that should be redrawing a whole page.
