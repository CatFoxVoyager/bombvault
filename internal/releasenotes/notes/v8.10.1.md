The green flash is fixed at its cause this time, not moved out of the default.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **The page entrance no longer scales or tilts** ([#228](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/228)). v8.10.0 moved the default down to "Subtle", where neither happens. That kept a new install away from the bug and left it untouched for anybody who had chosen "Wild", because a changed default never reaches a setting you picked yourself. This release removes the cause instead.
- **Why those two were the cause.** A page that slides can be drawn independently of its contents. A page that scales or rotates cannot: the browser has to redraw everything inside it through the page's own transform, and the cards and rows in there are animating too. On Firefox/macOS the coloured badges were drawn from uninitialised graphics memory, which is the `#38FF38` green; Chromium got the colour right and showed the same double redraw as trembling.
- **"Wild" is still clearly livelier than "Subtle"**: three times the travel, longer, on a curve that overshoots and settles. None of that asks the browser to redraw a page.
- **"Storm" lost the largest values in the file** (3 degrees, 90%). Since it began overriding the system's reduced-motion setting in v8.10.0, it is the one level that animates for somebody who asked for less movement, so it is the last place that should be redrawing a whole page.
